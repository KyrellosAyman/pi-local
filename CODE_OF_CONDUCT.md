# Code of conduct

Treat other participants with respect. Discuss the work, evidence, and tradeoffs. Give specific, constructive feedback and credit other people’s work.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, threats, persistent personal attacks, and sharing someone’s private information without consent are unacceptable in this project’s issues, pull requests, or other community spaces.

Report abusive content through GitHub’s reporting tools or contact the maintainer privately. The maintainer may remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts. The maintainer will handle reports discreetly.
