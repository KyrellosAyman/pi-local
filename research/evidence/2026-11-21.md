## Long-workflow measurements

One guided S01 repair lasted 8,787.78 seconds. The timed trace matched all 160 main requests and nine compactions. Median native decode was 19.21 tokens/s. Of 160 requests, 103 fell below 20 and none reached 40. The largest observed slot use was 43,362 of 64,000 tokens. Median post-tool wait was 1.47 seconds, the longest was 248.63 seconds, and 0 of 2,390 host samples reached 95% CPU and RAM together. Keep actual work time, model decode, and requested context separate in further comparisons.

The recovered follow-up matched 85 desktop requests and four of five compactions. Median decode was 22.04 tokens/s, 13 requests fell below 20, and the maximum main input was 39,450 tokens. Of the longest 200.623-second post-tool wait, 158.035 seconds were compaction and 42.139 seconds were native prefill. No sample reached 95% CPU and RAM together in 1,307 observations.

The uncoached installed arm matched 117 desktop requests and all 16 compactions in 100 minutes. Median decode was 22.33 tokens/s, 13 requests fell below 20, and none reached 40. The largest input was 41,275 tokens. Of the longest 171.193-second post-tool delay, 101.925 seconds were compaction and 68.731 seconds were native prefill. Tk received the first update in 40 ms. None of 1,714 host samples reached 95% CPU and RAM together.
